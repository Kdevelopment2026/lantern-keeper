Lantern Keeper

Put your phone down. Keep the light on.

A calm ritual for the moment "one more video" becomes another lost hour. Choose how long to keep watch, turn your phone face down, and a lighthouse keeps the light on until morning. When you return, the log shows the time you kept.

Lantern Keeper measures time away from your phone. It does not track sleep, block other apps, or keep score. There are no streaks to lose.

No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your iPhone.
