Lantern Keeper is a calm ritual for the moment "one more video" becomes another lost hour. Choose how long to keep watch, turn your phone face down, and a lighthouse keeps the light on until morning. When you return, the log shows the time you kept.

No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your phone.

ONE RITUAL, NOT A DASHBOARD
Open the app, tap Begin watch, turn the phone over. That is the whole thing. The lantern warms, the beam sweeps once across the water, and the watch is set. Lock the phone and leave it.

HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT MEASURES
Lantern Keeper records when a watch began, when it was planned to end, and whether you ended it early. It measures time away from your phone. It does not track sleep, score your night, or claim to know what happened after you put the phone down.

NO SHAME, NO STREAKS
Ending a watch early is logged as exactly that — ended early — and the morning log says "No judgement. Begin again tonight." There are no streaks to lose, no red failure states, no points.

MORNING, NOT METRICS
At the planned end time the palette turns to dawn. You see the watch time kept, a ship for every completed watch, and an optional one-word reflection: clear, steady, or tired. Add a note if you like.

A QUIET LOGBOOK
The last seven nights appear as a shoreline of marks, with total watch time underneath. Delete any entry, or everything, whenever you want.

MADE FOR THE NIGHT
• A single lighthouse scene with a real rotating lens, drawn natively
• Optional morning notification — asked for only when you turn it on
• The face-down gesture is optional; a visible Start button always works
• VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and Increased Contrast supported
• Works fully offline; no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind

Lantern Keeper does not block other apps and is not a medical or sleep product. It helps you put the phone down; it does not pretend to know what happens after.
